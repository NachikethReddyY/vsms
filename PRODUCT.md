# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Event staff running community vision-screening events: Registration Officers, Screeners, Reviewers, and Event Managers. They need to move each participant reliably through registration, QR check-in, screening stations, review, and hand-off during a live event.

## Product Purpose

VSMS records community vision-screening results and guides staff through each participant's next step. Success is a clean, tablet-friendly interface that makes completion, pass/follow-up status, the next participant, and QR scanning obvious while preserving results offline and synchronising them when connected.

## Positioning

The event-day workflow for capturing vision-screening results, continuing safely offline, and using QR passes to keep each participant moving simply.

## Brand Personality

Clean, visual, easy, and user-friendly. Calm operational focus with enough colour to make important states and next actions immediately clear.

## Anti-references

Avoid vibe-coded decoration, tiny tablet targets, consumer-app styling, generic hospital portals, boring SaaS dashboards, and AI-interface tells. Do not copy Ollama's lack of colour; borrow the clean, focused feel associated with Cursor, OpenAI, OpenCode, and the restrained warmth of Claude Code.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious at every station.
- Let colour explain operational state, never decorate empty space.
- Keep controls large and direct enough for busy tablet use.
- Preserve continuity: save locally first, then communicate sync state clearly.
- Use QR passes to remove lookup friction without exposing unnecessary participant information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements were specified. Tablet-friendly touch targets are required; animations may be used to clarify state changes.
