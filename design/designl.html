Visual Screening Management System

Run community vision screening
without paper, queues, or guesswork.

VSMS is the event-operations system for vision-screening teams. Register participants, move them through stations, surface rule-based flags to a reviewer, and keep every record safe, even when the venue has no connection.

The problem with screening day

Community screening events are clinically sound and operationally fragile. The failure is never the test. It is everything around it.

a Paper forms slow every station Results are handwritten, re-entered later, and occasionally lost. A participant's record exists in three places and agrees with itself in none of them.
b Queues are managed by shouting Staff walk participants between stations from memory. Nobody knows who is waiting where, who has been seen, or why one station has stopped moving.
c Reporting arrives weeks late Outcomes are assembled into spreadsheets after the event. Flags that needed same-day review surface when the reviewer has gone home.

One participant, one continuous record

Every participant moves through the same stations. Every station reads and writes the same record. The queue is the interface.

Registration
Find or register, record consent, check in, assign the first queue.
Visual acuity
Distance and near acuity recorded against configured thresholds.
Refraction
Objective refraction results captured at the station, on the spot.
Colour vision
Screening plates recorded pass / refer, never interpreted in-app.
Eye health
External eye health observations recorded for the reviewer.
Clinical review
A reviewer inspects every flag, makes the final decision, creates the referral.

Built for church halls, not server rooms

Screening events happen in venues with no reliable connection. VSMS treats offline as a normal working condition, not an error state.

Every record is saved on the device first. Staff see one calm message: "Saved safely on this device. Records will synchronise when the connection returns." Work continues; nothing is lost.

When the connection returns, synchronisation is explicit and observable. Staff always know which records are where, and conflicts are surfaced to a human. They are never silently resolved.

QR event passes let staff pull up a participant in one scan. The code carries an event identifier only: never names, results, or clinical data.

Five roles, one workspace

Every staff action is permissioned. Each role sees the same event through the lens of its own work.

Registration Officer Finds or registers participants, records consent, creates the event check-in, assigns the first queue.
Screener Scans the next participant, enters station results, acknowledges flags, transfers to the next station.
Reviewer Inspects flagged results, makes the final decision, records notes, creates referrals and urgent escalations.
Event Manager Watches queues, bottlenecks, event progress, staffing, and sync health across the whole venue.
Administrator Manages users, roles, events, stations, thresholds, and audit access.

The live queue is the product

One continuous workspace: who is waiting, at which station, for how long, and what needs a reviewer. This is a reduced preview; the prototype is fully interactive.
